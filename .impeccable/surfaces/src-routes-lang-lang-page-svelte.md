# Landing page

Scope: the marketing landing page, one route in two locales (`/` Indonesian, `/en` English). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a deal lead, founder, or lawyer in Indonesia, mid-deal, sharing documents by cloud-drive link, deciding whether Rakda is trustworthy enough to move the deal into.

Action: start the 30-day trial via the env-configured app URL. Secondary: sign in, language switch.

Proof carried: the live permission grid on the real M&A template folders, watermark burned per view with timestamp and IP, append-only activity, group-siloed Q&A, five templates, archive package, Jakarta storage, AES-256 at rest with provider-held key. Demonstration data synthetic and labeled. No prices, customers, logos, or compliance badges.

Memorable moment: flipping a cell and watching the watermark burn and the activity line append before the finger lifts. Grid state in the URL.

Raises kept: recency as ink darkness; consequence drawn the instant it happens; state in the URL.

Section grammar (user decision, 2026-09-05): every section after the hero uses one pattern, the intro column left and sticky, the content column right, stacked below 1024px. The earlier raise "no two sections composed alike" is withdrawn; consistency across sections outranks it.

Unresolved: deploy target; production app URL; group names localized per locale (yes); demo IP is a documentation-range placeholder (yes); teal under 10 percent of any screen (kept).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page is Rakda's access grid itself; the visitor decides who sees what in the first viewport. It refuses the hero-screenshot-then-feature-grid arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: The app's Clean Room inherited: cool near-white surfaces; slate-teal only where it means action, permission, or watermark; Inter for prose, JetBrains Mono for every machine fact; 1px hairlines as the page's material; flat, light only, radii 6 to 8px.

STORY: A deal lead sharing files by drive link sees a real permission grid, flips a cell, watches the watermark burn and the log append, believes control is this concrete, and starts the trial.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim nav: mark, id/en, sign in, trial. A band: headline, one-line offer, trial button. Below, full width: live matrix, rows the M&A template folders, columns three guest groups, cells working toggles; right, a document preview with a burned watermark and an appending activity line, newest darkest.

FORM: The Permission Matrix, structure 3 of 7, grounded, code-led; seed key fd760216.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
